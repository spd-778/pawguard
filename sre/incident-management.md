## Severity

### SEV-1

Complete service outage.

### SEV-2

Major degradation affecting important functionality.

### SEV-3

Minor degradation with limited impact.

## Postmortem

Significant incidents should document:

- Timeline
- Customer impact
- Root cause
- Detection
- Mitigation
- Recovery
- Corrective actions
